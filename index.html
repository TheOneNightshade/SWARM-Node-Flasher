The flasher only works on a secure page. Open it from an https:// address or from http://localhost.
Seeed Xiao ESP32-C5

How it goes

  1. Plug the board into this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click Connect & install and pick the board's port in the popup (usually "USB JTAG/serial debug unit" or a COM / cu.usbmodem port).
  3. Click Install. On the Erase device screen, leave the box unticked to keep the node's number, scan plan and stored sightings. Tick it only for a fresh start or when the board ran other firmware that won't boot. Erasing also frees the node from its owner phone.
  4. Wait for it to finish (about a minute), then choose Logs & Console and press the board's reset button. You should see: 
    link: broadcast + direct
swarm node build 20
firmware slot app0, Bluetooth updates possible
no owner yet: the first Swarm app to connect becomes the owner
swarm node ready: ..., node 0, ...
    Then open the Swarm app near the board: it locks the node to your phone the first time they connect. Other people's Swarm apps can't use it after that.

If it won't connect

  • No port in the list: try another cable (many are charge-only) or another USB port.
  • Port shows but it fails to connect: put the board in download mode. Hold BOOT, tap RESET (or plug it in while holding BOOT), then let go and try again. Press RESET once after flashing.
  • Port is busy: close the Arduino IDE Serial Monitor or any other app using the board.
  • Old firmware boot-loops after flashing: flash again with Erase device ticked.
