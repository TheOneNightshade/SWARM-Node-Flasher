Waveshare ESP32-C5-WiFi6-KIT

Step by step · Flashes Swarm node build 23

1 · What you need

  • The Waveshare ESP32-C5-WiFi6-KIT (any flash / PSRAM version).
  • A USB-C data cable. Lots of cables only charge and won't work.
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS). Phones, Firefox and Safari can't flash boards.

2 · Install the USB driver (Windows and Mac)

The kit uses a CH343 USB-serial chip. If your computer doesn't already know it, install Waveshare's driver first, then restart the browser:

  • Windows: CH343SER.7z
  • Mac: CH34XSER_MAC.7z
  • Linux and ChromeOS: nothing to install.

3 · Plug into the UART port

The kit has two USB-C ports. Use the one marked UART, and plug it straight into the computer, not through a hub.

The UART port resets the board into flashing mode by itself, so you don't need to press any buttons, and it's the only port that shows the node's log after flashing.

4 · Install

This browser can't talk to USB serial devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS).
The flasher only works on a secure page. Open it from an https:// address or from http://localhost.
Swarm node for ESP32-C5 DevKit-style boards (right one for this kit)
  1. Click Connect & install.
  2. In the popup pick the CH343 port. It's usually called USB-Enhanced-SERIAL CH343, a COM port on Windows, or cu.wchusbserial… / cu.usbmodem… on a Mac.
  3. Click Install. On the Erase device screen, tick the box the first time you put Swarm on this board. Leave it unticked when you're only updating a board that already runs Swarm.
  4. Wait. Erasing the kit's large flash and writing the firmware takes a few minutes, so let it run even if the progress bar looks stuck.

5 · Check it's running

  1. When it says it's done, choose Logs & Console.
  2. Press the kit's RESET button. You should see: 
    swarm node build 23
firmware slot app0, Bluetooth updates possible
no owner yet: the first Swarm app to connect becomes the owner
swarm node ready: ..., node 0, ...

6 · Pair it with your phone

  1. Open the Swarm app near the kit. It finds the node and sets it up.
  2. The first time they connect, the node locks to your phone. Other people's Swarm apps can't use it after that.
  3. To free the node for someone else, hold the kit's BOOT button for 5 seconds while it's running.

If it won't flash

  • No port in the list: install the driver from step 2, then try another data cable or USB port.
  • "Failed to initialize" or it won't connect: put the kit in flashing mode by hand. Hold BOOT, press and let go of RESET, then let go of BOOT. Click Connect & install again and pick the port fresh from the list.
  • Port is busy: close the Arduino IDE Serial Monitor, other tabs of this page, or any other app using the board.
  • It keeps dropping off USB: the kit needs a solid 5 V. Plug straight into the computer, not into a hub, front-panel port or long extension.
  • Flashed, but the console stays blank: you're probably on the other USB-C port. Move the cable to UART, open the console and press RESET.
  • Boot-loops after flashing: flash again with Erase device ticked.
